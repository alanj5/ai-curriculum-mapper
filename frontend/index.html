AI Curriculum Mapper — quick guide

This tool maps Imperial Computing modules to the 18 ACM/IEEE CS2023 Knowledge Areas so students and staff can see how the curriculum fits together. Every mapping is an AI-generated suggestion for decision-support — you stay in control.

🧭 Overview (home)

Your starting point: the headline numbers, the best-covered and thinnest areas, and a one-click jump into the Curriculum Map.

🕸 Curriculum Map (the centrepiece)

See how modules and concepts relate as an interactive graph. Switch views, filter by programme or year, trace prerequisite chains, and spot clusters.

🔍 Explore

Search modules or concepts; open any one to see its outcomes, topics, prerequisites and where each concept is taught.

📊 Coverage

How well each Knowledge Area and programme outcome is covered across the curriculum, with the gaps highlighted.

✓ Review (educators)

Validate the AI's suggestions: accept, reject or reassign any mapping. Every change is undoable.
